One image summing up 26 November 2026's world news as a newspaper front
page: new U.S. tariffs, the Hormuz tanker incident, the Netanyahu trial,
the Giza fuel-truck fire, UK net migration, and the delayed Macy's parade.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126

`render.py` draws `news-frontpage.png` (1024×1024) with PIL;
`main.py` (standard library only) copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
